The EchoFiles logo: three stacked sheets (ember, violet, sky) on a navy tile, the front sheet forming an E-shaped folder.

- `echofiles-logo.webp` is the original artwork, the source of truth.
- `echofiles-logo.svg` is a vector redraw of it for the Linux app icon (`hicolor/scalable/apps`) and small sizes; its colours are baked in (gradients from `brand-ember`, `brand-violet`, `brand-sky` on `brand-navy`).
- Use only for the app icon, About and onboarding. Never inside the working UI, never recoloured, never on a light ground without its navy tile.
